Where the GB200 NVL72's "1.44 exaFLOPS" comes from

The chapter quotes 1.44 exaFLOPS for FP4 and 720 petaFLOPS for FP8. Both are NVIDIA datasheet peaks: one Blackwell GPU's rated tensor-core throughput, doubled by 2:1 structured sparsity, multiplied by the 72 GPUs in the rack.
