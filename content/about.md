## Bio

I am a Senior Software Engineer in the Inference team at CoreWeave. I have been involved in the Kubernetes space since Kubernetes 1.3 and have worked for Microsoft, [Kinvolk](https://twitter.com/kinvolkio), and Red Hat on various Kubernetes technologies such as OpenShift, service-meshes, Helm, Lokomotive, etc. I have also contributed to several Kubernetes upstream projects, including Kubernetes, Kubernetes docs, Contour, Podman, and Crio. I hold CKA and CKS certifications, and I was also an organizer of the Kubernetes Bangalore meetup group from 2017 to 2022. I have spoken at numerous conferences and events, such as Kubecon, Pycon India, DevOpsDays Bangalore, FOSDEM, Cloud Native Rejekts, and DevConf.

I am a bibliophile, tech enthusiast, open-source contributor, productivity geek, hiker, etc.
